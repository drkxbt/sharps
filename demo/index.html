Follow the money on Polymarket

Where the top wallets on Polymarket have their money right now: the markets they're piling into, what they paid and how one-sided they are. Every large trade as it happens, with a star when it comes from a trader on the board. Open any wallet for what it holds, its profit over time and its record, or browse the board by category and window. Everything comes from Polymarket's public API, with no key and no account. Free and MIT licensed.
